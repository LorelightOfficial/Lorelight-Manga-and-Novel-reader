# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Lorelight to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this project:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for users and the project
- Remembering that maintainers and contributors are volunteers

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, including demanding free support
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub, by opening a private security advisory or contacting [@LorelightOfficial](https://github.com/LorelightOfficial). All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.
